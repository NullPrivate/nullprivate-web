If you prefer not to enable system-wide encrypted DNS, you can configure encrypted DNS only in the browser. Chromium 79+ supports `DoH`. Below are the steps for Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, 360, QQ, etc.):

1. Open Chrome’s `Settings`
1. Navigate to `Privacy, search, and services`
1. Scroll down to `Security`
1. Enable `Use secure DNS to specify how to look up the network address of websites`
1. Under `Choose a service provider`, enter `https://public.adguardprivate.com/dns-query`
