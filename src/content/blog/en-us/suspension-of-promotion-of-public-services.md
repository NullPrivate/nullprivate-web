title: "Suspend Promotion of Public Services"
pubDate: "2025-03-16 10:35:32 +0800"

tags: [Operations, blog]
---

The original purpose of the public server is to allow users to experience our services without any barriers, thereby switching to paid services. However, since the blocking effect of the public service has already met the needs of most users, many users have been using the free public service for a long time, and new users are still continuously accessing it. This occupies a large amount of server resources and increases our operating costs. In order to ensure service quality, AdGuardPrivate has decided not to expand or promote the free public servers anymore. We strongly recommend that users migrate to paid services to enjoy more stable and efficient DNS resolution services. Paid services not only provide higher performance and reliability but also include more advanced features and technical support.

Thank you for your understanding and support.
